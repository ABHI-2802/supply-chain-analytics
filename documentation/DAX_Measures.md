# 📐 DAX Measures — Supply Chain Performance Dashboard

> All DAX measures used in the Power BI dashboard, organised by category.

---

## 📊 KPI Card Measures

### Total Revenue
```dax
Total Revenue = SUM('clean data'[Revenue generated])
```
**Purpose:** Aggregates revenue across all 100 products.  
**Format:** Currency (₹) with 2 decimal places → displayed as **₹577.60K**

---

### Total Orders
```dax
Total Orders = COUNTROWS('clean data')
```
**Purpose:** Counts the total number of product/order records.  
**Result:** **100**

---

### Average Defect Rate
```dax
Avg Defect Rate = AVERAGE('clean data'[Defect rate (clean)])
```
**Purpose:** Calculates the mean defect rate across all products.  
**Format:** Percentage with 2 decimal places → **2.28%**

---

### Average Shipping Time
```dax
Avg Shipping Time = AVERAGE('clean data'[Shipping times])
```
**Purpose:** Calculates the mean shipping duration across all orders.  
**Format:** Number with 2 decimal places → **5.75 days**

---

## 💰 Revenue Analysis Measures

### Revenue by Product Type
```dax
Revenue by Product Type = 
CALCULATE(
    SUM('clean data'[Revenue generated]),
    ALLEXCEPT('clean data', 'clean data'[Product type])
)
```
**Purpose:** Revenue breakdown by product category (cosmetics, haircare, skincare).  
**Used in:** Horizontal bar chart — "Sum of Revenue generated by Product type"

---

### Revenue by Revenue Tier
```dax
Revenue by Tier = 
CALCULATE(
    SUM('analysis'[Revenue generated]),
    ALLEXCEPT('analysis', 'analysis'[Revenue tier])
)
```
**Purpose:** Revenue segmented by High / Medium / Low tiers.  
**Used in:** Donut chart — "Sum of Revenue generated by Revenue tier"

---

### Revenue by Transportation Mode
```dax
Revenue by Transport = 
CALCULATE(
    SUM('clean data'[Revenue generated]),
    ALLEXCEPT('clean data', 'clean data'[Transportation modes], 'clean data'[Product type])
)
```
**Purpose:** Revenue split by transport mode (Rail, Road, Air, Sea) and product type.  
**Used in:** Stacked bar chart — "Sum of Revenue generated by Transportation modes and Product type"

---

```dax
Shipping by Delivery Status = 
CALCULATE(
```

## 🔧 Calculated Columns (in Analysis Sheet / Power BI)

These calculated fields were created either in Excel (analysis sheet) or as Power BI calculated columns:

### Delivery Status
```dax
Delivery Status = 
IF(
    'analysis'[Shipping times] <= 'analysis'[Lead times],
    "On Time",
    "Late"
)
```

### Stock Status
```dax
Stock Status = 
IF(
    'analysis'[Stock levels] > 10,
    "OK",
    "Critical"
)
```

### Manufacturing Efficiency
```dax
Mfg Efficiency = 
IF(
    'analysis'[Manufacturing lead time] <= 'analysis'[Lead time],
    "Within lead time",
    "Exceeds lead time"
)
```

### Revenue Tier
```dax
Revenue Tier = 
SWITCH(
    TRUE(),
    'analysis'[Revenue generated] >= 7000, "High",
    'analysis'[Revenue generated] >= 3000, "Medium",
    "Low"
)
```
